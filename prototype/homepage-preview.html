04 The rest of the vault

Everything else you need.

The daily workflows around the record: finding, comparing, cleaning up, and keeping it local.

Find anything

Global search, /model lookup, field filters, and measurement ranges. Save the question as a Smart Collection with a live count.

  • Model names and model numbers
  • Brand, steel, lock, shape, carry
  • Pinned sidebar and pinned-first ordering

Records that hold everything

Specifications, price, provenance, notes, custom fields, and a multi-image gallery. Choose the cover and keep the source page.

  • Text, number, and date fields
  • Multi-image upload and fullscreen gallery
  • Card fields and pinned sidebar parity

Compare with context

Named shortlists keep their own membership. Hide what matches, then print or export the matrix as a landscape PDF.

  • Differences-only view
  • Add a knife to several lists
  • Print or export as PDF

Know your data

Collection pulse shows the shape of the vault. Data completeness and measurement review show the records that need another pass.

  • Missing fields and coverage gaps
  • Guided measurement review
  • Label consistency and source review

Care history

Sharpening, cleaning, lubrication, stropping, and service details stay with the knife. Logs keep the change history.

  • Dated maintenance events
  • Grit, angle, system, and passes
  • Searchable activity logs

Local-first, backed up

SQLite and images in one folder. Create a portable ZIP backup, and opt into cloud backup only when you want it.

  • No account, works offline
  • Complete local ZIP restore
  • Opt-in cloud upload and restore
